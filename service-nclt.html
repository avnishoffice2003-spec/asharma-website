Practice Area

Litigation & NCLT Representation

Time-Sensitive: Insolvency Proceedings

If you have received a Demand Notice under Section 8 of the IBC, or if a petition has been filed against your company at the NCLT, you have a strictly limited window to respond before the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) is initiated. Immediate legal intervention is required to protect the Board of Directors.

Defending Corporate Interests

When internal disputes threaten the survival of a company, or when insolvency proceedings are initiated, you need aggressive and strategic representation at the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT). Led by CS Avnish Sharma, our litigation wing is designed to protect structural integrity, defend promoter rights, and resolve complex corporate deadlocks.

We do not just file petitions; we engineer litigation strategies that force settlements, protect assets, and ensure business continuity during hostile proceedings.

Tribunal Expertise

  • Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC)

    Filing and defending petitions under Sections 7, 9, and 10 of the IBC. We represent Financial Creditors, Operational Creditors, and Corporate Debtors throughout the CIRP.

  • Oppression & Mismanagement

    Strategic representation under Sections 241 & 242 of the Companies Act. We protect minority shareholders against oppressive majority actions and defend majority boards against frivolous claims.

  • Corporate Restructuring (M&A)

    Drafting, filing, and executing Schemes of Arrangement, Amalgamation, and Demerger under Sections 230-232, securing seamless NCLT approval.

  • Restoration of Struck-off Companies

    Aggressive appellate work under Section 252 to revive companies struck off by the ROC, securing active status to protect assets and ongoing operations.

The Appellate Advantage

Our capabilities extend beyond the NCLT. We provide appellate representation at the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT), ensuring that adverse tribunal orders are aggressively challenged and stayed when necessary to protect client interests.
